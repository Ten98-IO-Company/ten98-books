# Voucher entry

The voucher entry screen, slice 1: payment, receipt, contra and journal, created by keyboard, in single or double entry, with the bill panel, the working date and the number and balance previews. Altering, voids, deletes, duplicates, optional and post-dated vouchers (slice 3) and the invoice types (slice 2) are not built. Keys are in [KEYBOARD.md](KEYBOARD.md), "Voucher entry"; the reads the screen calls are in [LEDGER.md](LEDGER.md), "Voucher entry reads".

Code: the route `src/routes/app/c.$companyId.vouchers.tsx`, the screen in `src/components/vouchers/`, and every decision as a pure function in `src/lib/voucher-entry/` (amounts and dates, layouts and lists, bills, the body and its checks, the Enter path and the draft, refusals) and `src/lib/working-date.ts` (tested in `tests/voucher-entry.test.ts`; the draft's state machine in `tests/voucher-draft.test.ts`); bodies and the pending-bill order are tested over HTTP in `tests/voucher-entry.integration.ts`.

## Opening and types

- Company menu `V` (writers only: viewers get no Vouchers item on the menu, in the go-to list or in the sidebar, and a viewer who opens the address gets a notice whose button, focused, opens the day book) opens the type last used in this tab, payment the first time. `?type=` opens a type.
- The type is in the page header with the expected number ("No. 6 (expected)"); it is not a field. The type keys switch it in place. The date, lines and narration are kept and laid out again, not cleared and not re-checked (observed); the new type's rules apply at saving.
- **How a switch lays the lines out** (`relayLines`). No line ever changes side or amount and no bill row is dropped; a switch that cannot keep that is refused, the voucher stays as it is, and the status line says why.
  - Single entry to double entry of another type (observed): the Account first, on the new type's first-line side, with the total; the particulars keep their sides; a line on the short side follows when it does not balance. Observed: a payment of Bank, Expense 7 became a journal of Dr Bank 7, Dr Expense 7 and an empty Cr line.
  - The layout key on the same type: the particulars, then the Account's line on the Account's own side with the total (the body's order), so switching back gives the same voucher.
  - Double entry to single entry: the Account is the first line already on the new type's Account side whose ledger the Account field offers (cash or bank); every other line must be on the other side and add up to it. Otherwise (no such line, a second line on the Account's side, lines that do not balance, an unfinished line, or bill rows on the line that would become the Account) the switch is refused.
  - Single entry to single entry when the two types put the Account on different sides (payment and receipt): the voucher is laid out in double entry of the new type, every side kept. Receipt and contra share their sides and stay in single entry.
- Layouts: payment, receipt and contra open in single entry; the journal is double entry only. A layout chosen with the layout key holds for all three in this tab, for this user and company (`voucherMode:<userId>:<companyId>:cash` in session storage); blocked storage falls back to single entry. The type last used is kept the same way (`voucherType:<userId>:<companyId>`).

## The Enter path

- **Single entry**: Account (cash and bank only) → each line's ledger → amount → the bill panel on a bill-wise ledger → Enter on the blank line ends the list → Total, when the Account is not a cash-in-hand ledger → Narration → Enter asks to save. With a cash Account the blank line goes straight to Narration (observed in the key script: the total stop leads to the bank details, which a cash account does not have).
- **Double entry**: each line's side (the first line's is fixed by the type: debit on payment, journal and contra, credit on receipt; a line that became first otherwise, by a removal or a switch, keeps its side editable), ledger and amount. After an amount, Enter goes to Narration when the lines balance; otherwise a new line opens on the short side with the counter ledger of the last line's ledger from history (the most recently entered voucher of the type, read when that ledger is chosen) and the difference; a ledger with no history gets an empty line with the side proposed, and the difference comes once a ledger is chosen. Single entry proposes nothing (observed).
- **Saving**: the question takes Enter or `Y` for yes and `N` or Escape for no, which returns to the Account (single entry) or the first line's ledger (double entry) with everything kept. The accept key saves from anywhere without asking. After saving, the next voucher has the same type, layout and date and nothing else, and the cursor is on its first field.
- **Single-entry contra**: the Account is the receiving side (debited), the lines the giving side, as observed and as the starter dataset's lines are ordered (design revision 5 said credited; corrected).
- **Cash or bank Account** is decided by the ledger's group as the server reports it: cash in hand is the seeded cash-in-hand group and its sub-groups (`cashInHandIds`), never a name. *Assumption, not observed*: a single-entry contra whose Account is cash while a bank ledger sits on a line (the reference may open that line's bank pop-up) follows the rule by the Account and goes straight to Narration.
- **The new line's side** after an unbalanced amount is the short side: two of the four readings of the notes say so, and the other two read as debit; it is kept as designed until it is observed again.

## Amounts, dates and numbers

- Amounts are rounded half up to the company's places at entry; a value that rounds to zero empties the cell and the cursor stays there ("Enter an amount above zero."), so no line exists. Arabic-Indic digits and grouping commas are accepted. Signs and amounts beyond the stored range are errors in the cell.
- Dates are typed day first: `9`, `10-8`, `11/9/26`, `14.6.26`, `15-10-2026`, `12 sep`, `18-dec`; missing parts come from the voucher's date, a two-digit year is 20yy, and `2026-01-31` is accepted too. Refused: `130526`, `35`, `31-2`, `sep 16`. A refused date keeps the field open with a short message (a kept difference: the reference shows none).
- Bounds: not before the books begin, not inside the locked period, and not after the end of the financial year that holds the tab's working date.
- **Working date**: per tab, user and company (`workingDate:<userId>:<companyId>` in session storage; a kept date before the books begin is ignored), starting from the last voucher date (the books beginning while there is none). It follows the company menu's working date key, a day chosen in the day book (not the default day the day book opens on), the voucher date key (even without saving) and every saved voucher; a save also drops the reports' cached last voucher date, so the day book then opens on the new day. A new voucher opens on it. The company menu shows it and sets it to any date from the books beginning, which is how a tab enters the next financial year.
- The header shows the number the voucher is expected to take on its date; it is refreshed after a type or date change and after saving, and the saved message says so when another voucher took the number first.
- **Balances**: under the Account, "Current balance" and, on every line, the ledger's resulting balance at the date with the draft applied, all from the server's preview; the screen adds nothing itself. A cash-in-hand ledger that the draft takes below zero raises a warning that never blocks; the Account's balance turns to the danger tone.

## Bills

A line on a bill-wise ledger opens the bill panel after its amount, also on Enter over an unchanged amount. The first row is "Against a bill" when the party has pending bills on the other side, else "On account" (observed; sales and purchase, later, start with a new bill). A picked bill proposes the lesser of what is left of the line and its pending amount; a larger amount than the bill's pending, or a row on the other side, is accepted (the server warns `bill_over_settled`), but rows that add up to more than the line, or to its other side, are refused in the panel and again before sending; a server refusal about bill rows reopens that line's panel. A line whose panel holds only its default, one on-account row for the whole line, sends no rows: the server keeps the amount on account itself, and the stored voucher equals the one the demo seeder stores (`tests/voucher-entry.integration.ts`). Enter on a row's last cell adds a row for the rest, against a bill again while pending bills remain, or closes the panel when the rows cover the line. The accept key accepts with the rest on account; Escape keeps the line's earlier rows. A new bill takes a credit period (`30`, `30 days`, `2 weeks`, `1 month`, or a due date) with the due date shown as the database computes it. Pending bills are listed by name, case and hyphens ignored, as observed (10, 9, A-020, A-100, a2, B-050, B1, b3, B-300, C-200).

## Escape, leaving and failures

- Escape empties the focused field or line cell first, prefilled values included; the next Escape asks to leave when anything was entered, and No keeps the cursor where it was. The quit key asks at once when anything was entered. Both ask also in a balanced voucher, which the reference leaves silently: a kept difference, because that loses a complete voucher without warning. Leaving by any route (go-to, a link, the browser) asks the same; text typed in a line cell and not committed yet counts as entered (it is committed first).
- The difference of a double-entry voucher has its own status line; a refused switch says why on another, which stays until the next switch or save.
- Before sending, the screen refuses what would otherwise be lost or refused (`bodyProblems`): a line with something in it but no ledger, amount or side; a bill row with no method, amount or bill, or with a credit period that does not parse; totals beyond the stored range; the server's own limits on lines and narration, checked with its schema; then the Account, balance, the cash and bank rule of payment, receipt and contra, and the date bounds. Server refusals are placed by their code (`refusalPlaces` in `src/lib/voucher-entry/refusals.ts`, total over the codes).
- **Keys and focus.** When a key moves the cursor, it has moved before the key's handler returns, so characters typed straight after land in the new field: the type keys, the layout switch, the date key and Enter in the date field, the accept key in the bill panel (the panel closes and the cursor goes on at once; the panel's own return of focus to the grid one task later is undone while the cursor is held, and the hold ends the moment the user presses any key, so a key pressed before that return can still lose the cursor to it: the shared dialog has no way yet to be told where focus returns on close), and the screen's first field as it appears. While a save runs the cursor waits on the "Saving…" line, which is named and not editable: keys typed then are not kept anywhere (never in a read-only field), and the next voucher's first field takes the cursor the moment the answer comes. Keys typed while the page is still loading are not kept either; the page says it is loading. The accept key is claimed in every state, so classic Ctrl+A never selects the page; while the voucher is sending, unknown or in conflict it does nothing.
- The leave and quit question is the application's confirmation, opening on its cancel button, so Enter keeps the voucher: Y and N do not answer it yet (a parity gap; the reference answers it with Y and N). The save question is the masters' inline question, which Enter and Y answer yes and N and Escape no.

## The draft, its key and its lock

The voucher on screen is one state machine (`step` in `src/lib/voucher-entry/draft-machine.ts`, run by `createDraftController` in `draft-controller.ts`, which the screen's `useVoucherDraft` binds to the API and the tab's session storage). Its content, its idempotency key, the state of its save and the drafts kept across a lost session live there and nowhere else; the screen's lock, alerts and commands are read from it.

- **The key rule.** A draft has one key from its creation until a voucher is known saved under it or the user discards the draft. A refusal, an edit, an unknown outcome or a restore never changes it. This is safe because the server writes the key only with a committed voucher ([LEDGER.md](LEDGER.md), idempotency): a refused body leaves no trace of the key, the same body again is a replay, and a different body under a used key is refused with 409 `idempotency_mismatch`, never a second voucher.
- **States.** `editing` (everything allowed), `sending` (one request in flight; a second accept is dropped), `unknown` (the send ended without a definite answer: the voucher may be in the books) and `conflict` (the server answered that the key is already used by a voucher with other content). Every change to the draft goes through the machine, which refuses it unless the state is `editing`: edits, the date, type and layout switches, lines and bills, checks before sending, and taking or dropping a kept draft. The cells, the Account and the narration are read-only and the draft commands are off while it is not `editing`, and Escape empties nothing then (it only asks to leave).
- **Answers** are classified once (`classifyAnswer`), and the class decides the transition:

  | Answer | Class | From `editing` | From `unknown` (a retry) |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 2xx | saved | a new draft, same type, layout and date, a new key | the same |
  | 409 `idempotency_mismatch` (the application's JSON) | conflict | `conflict` | `conflict` |
  | 403 or 404 from the application: another origin, a role or membership the company no longer allows (a viewer's or a removed member's write is answered 404 `not_found`), an unknown route | denied | editable again, the answer shown | stays `unknown`, the answer noted under the alert: these checks run before the replay, so they say nothing about the first send |
  | any other 4xx from the application, coded or not (validation, locked period, 405, 413, 415, a codeless 409) | refused | editable again, the message shown, the key kept | `editing`: the same body under the same key was not replayed, so no earlier send committed |
  | 401 | signed out | editable again; kept in the tab; the screen goes to signing in without the leave question | stays `unknown` (the answer noted), kept |
  | 429 | limited | editable again, the answer shown | stays `unknown`, the answer noted |
  | a 5xx, a 4xx that is not the application's own error (a proxy's page, or a proxy's JSON such as `{"message": ...}`, an array or a number), an unreadable answer, no answer | none | `unknown` | stays `unknown` |

  "From the application" means its own error shape: a JSON object whose `error` is a string, as every API route answers (`isOwnError` in `src/lib/api.ts`, which sets `ApiError.json`). So nothing that may have committed is ever classed refused or denied: a proxy that answers after the server committed leaves the voucher unknown and kept. The server is not changed to add codes or a marker header in this branch (a header set by the API would be stronger than the shape check: an open item).
- **Unknown.** The alert offers "Check and retry", which sends the same body under the same key (the server saves it once or replays it; the saved message then says the voucher was already in the books), and "Leave".
- **Conflict.** The voucher is kept as it was, frozen, with "Open the day book" (on its date), "Start a new voucher" (after the question; a new key, and the expected number read again) and "Leave".
- **Kept in the tab.** The tab's list (`voucherDrafts:<userId>:<companyId>` in session storage) holds the voucher on screen whenever it is `sending`, `unknown` or `conflict`, written the moment it gets there, with its content, key, state and the exact body sent. It is taken out on a save, on a confirmed discard, when the draft is refused or editable again, and when it is restored as editable. A lost session keeps an `editing` draft too. Session storage lives as long as the tab: the list survives leaving the screen, a reload, a crash of the page and the browser restoring the tab; it is not in a new tab opened on the same address, and a closed tab that the browser does not restore takes it along. So after leaving or a reload the voucher is offered back in the state that can be proved: a voucher left while `sending` comes back `unknown` (its answer never reached the tab), with "Check and retry" under its own key. A screen no longer shown never takes an entry out (a newer screen may have restored it): when its answer arrives it rewrites its own entry with what it learned, refused (the editable draft with the refusal) or saved: a notice "saved as No. N", offered with "Dismiss" only, never restored or retried, and never turned back into "may be saved" by another screen. Before a restore or a dismiss the screen rereads the list, so it acts on what the tab holds now. Several kept drafts are offered one after the other; one is restored or discarded only while the voucher on screen is `editing` and empty (otherwise the banner's buttons are off and say why), and never called unsaved when it may be saved. Kept drafts are read back only when every field checks.
- **The questions** (`src/lib/voucher-entry/questions.ts`). Leaving asks by the state: `editing` with anything entered: unsaved; `sending`: it may already be saved and is kept here, offered with "Check and retry" on return; `unknown`: the same, after no definite answer; `conflict`: not saved, kept here. The screen never leaves without the user's answer. While a save is on its way the question waits for the user; if the save's answer comes first: saved, the question closes and the screen stays with "Saved <kind> No. N" (the next press of the leave key leaves at once: nothing is unsaved); refused or editable again, the question closes and the screen shows why; unknown or in conflict, the question is replaced by the one for that state and still waits. The only navigation without an answer is the redirect to signing in on a lost session. After "Stay" the cursor returns to "Check and retry" when that is the way on, to the first field after a save, else where it was. "Start a new voucher" asks before it discards. "Discard it" on the banner asks for a kept voucher that may be saved, is in conflict, or holds typed work; "Dismiss" on a saved notice does not ask.
- **Tested** by the transition table, the lock tests and the questions on the machine, the controller and `questions.ts`; a model test (`tests/voucher-draft.test.ts`: seeds 20261115, 1 and 424242, 5,000 sequences each of up to 40 steps; `MODEL_SEEDS` and `MODEL_SEQUENCES` run others) against a fake server with only the server's three facts plus answers that never reach the replay, where a request reaches the server and its answer comes back at separate steps, so leaving, reloading, signing out, a second tab and a late answer happen while it is in flight. It checks: one intention never has two vouchers (I1); nothing changes in `sending`, `unknown` and `conflict` (I2); the key changes only on a save, a discard or a restore of its own key (I3); the alert shows exactly in `unknown` and `conflict` as the answers say (I4); from `unknown` a definite answer leaves it and one that never reached the replay does not (I5); a voucher left, reloaded or signed out while `sending`, `unknown` or `conflict` is offered on the next screen (I6); a kept entry never says its answer never reached the tab once an answer settling that save (saved, refused, conflict) reached a screen of it (I7). It asserts for each seed on its own that it reaches every case named in the test, and fails under each deliberate breakage of the rules above. Against the real server: a lost answer then a retry; unknown, the session deleted, signed in, restored and retried; a kept draft saved in one tab and changed in another (409, conflict, frozen) while the body unchanged is a replay; unknown, the member removed, retried (denied, still unknown); a proxy's JSON 403 or 404 after the commit (unknown, kept, the retry replays it); and every answer class the server can produce. Each is one voucher at most.

## Gaps

Not built in slice 1, each a parity gap until built:

- **Ledger in place**: altering the highlighted ledger from a lookup (`Ctrl+Enter` in classic, observed) and creating one there (`Alt+C`). Until then `Ctrl+Enter` saves the voucher there as everywhere (it is an accept key), and `Alt+C` is claimed and does nothing.
- **Lookup matching in the lines**: a line's ledger cell matches with the design system's entry grid rule: typed text is found in a ledger's name or second-language name, ignoring letter case, names starting with it first, then names holding it anywhere; the first match is highlighted as one types, and Enter or Tab picks the highlighted ledger (its id); with nothing highlighted, Enter takes only text equal to a name, and leaving the cell takes only an exact name, else the cell says "Not in the list". What it does not do: ignore spaces and the punctuation the name match key ignores, so "hy phen" and "hyphen" do not find "Hy-Phen" in a line. The Account field matches under the name match key (`filterChoices`, `resolveTyped`, as the master forms do). The grid will take a matching function from the design system; then the lines use the shared key too.
- **Lists opening on arrival**: the Account field (a header lookup) cannot open its list programmatically, so it opens on typing or Down; grid cells open theirs as observed. The resulting balance on a line cannot take the danger tone (the grid has no per-cell tone); the warning covers it.
- **Bank allocations**: the reference opens a bank pop-up between the Total and the Narration of a voucher with a bank Account, and after a bank line; not built, so the bank path is blank line, Total, Narration.
- **The quit question's letters**: Y and N do not answer it yet (see above).
- Cost-centre allocations from the panel list, narration per line, the rate and per columns, and the item invoice.
